# Research direction

## Research question
Can AI-assisted transformation improve accessibility and comprehension of STEM educational content while preserving essential technical information?

## Baselines
A. Original educational content
B. Basic AI transformation
C. Proposed accessibility + technical-fidelity workflow

## Candidate evaluation measures
- comprehension score
- information retention
- technical terminology preservation
- numerical/equation preservation
- time to answer
- perceived difficulty
- usability

## Important methodological point
Do not claim that the current heuristic fidelity score proves correctness. The research phase should define an annotation protocol and evaluate the metric against human/expert judgments.
